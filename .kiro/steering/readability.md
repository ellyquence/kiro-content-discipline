# Readability (Quick Reference)

## Targets
- Grade level: ≤ 8.0
- Reading ease: ≥ 60
- Sentence avg: 15-20 words (25 max)
- Paragraph: 2-4 sentences (5 max)
- Active voice: ≥ 90%

## Quick Fixes
- One idea per sentence
- Simple words (use not utilize, help not facilitate)
- Active voice ("Sarah wrote" not "was written by")
- Headers every 300 words
- Lists for 3+ items
