# SEO & E-E-A-T (Quick Reference)

## E-E-A-T Signals
- **Experience**: "I tested...", original data, specific results
- **Expertise**: Credentials, accurate terms, depth
- **Authority**: External citations, expert quotes
- **Trust**: Disclosures, sources, dates, contact

## SEO Checklist
- [ ] Title: 50-60 chars, keyword front-loaded
- [ ] Meta desc: 150-160 chars, value + CTA
- [ ] Keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] 3-5 internal links
- [ ] 2-5 external authoritative sources
