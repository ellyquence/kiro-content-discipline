# Voice & Tone (Quick Reference)

## Voice = WHO you are (consistent)
## Tone = HOW you express it (adapts to context)

## Voice Attributes
- **Formality**: Casual (contractions, conversational)
- **Enthusiasm**: Measured (not overly excited)
- **Humor**: Playful when natural (wit, not jokes)
- **Authority**: Peer-expert blend ("Here's what I found")

## Writing Rules
- Use active voice (90%+)
- Second person ("you")
- Contractions naturally
- Short sentences (15-20 words avg)
- Short paragraphs (3-4 sentences)

## Avoid
- Buzzwords without substance
- Excessive exclamation points
- Corporate speak
- Filler phrases ("in order to", "due to the fact that")
