# Headlines (Quick Reference)

## Rules
- Write 25+ variations before selecting
- 6-13 words, 50-60 chars max
- Score min 35/60 (Clarity, Specificity, Problem, Benefit, Curiosity, Uniqueness)

## Top Formulas
- How-To: How to [Outcome]
- List: [#] Ways to [Outcome] (use odd numbers)
- Question: [Audience Question]?
- Problem+Benefit: [Problem]? [Benefit Solution]

## Avoid
- Clickbait ("You Won't Believe...")
- Starting with "I" or "My"
- Vague promises
